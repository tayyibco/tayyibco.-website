Founder's Story — How TAYYIB CO. Began

Tayyib Co. began with something deeply personal.

Like many others, I loved wearing lipsticks — trying different shades, following trends, and expressing myself through beauty. But over time, I started noticing something wasn’t right. My lips became extremely dry, sensitive, and prone to irritation. What seemed like a small issue slowly turned into a recurring problem.

I experienced persistent dryness, discomfort, and at times even inflammation — symptoms commonly associated with conditions like contact cheilitis, which can be triggered by certain cosmetic ingredients.

That was the moment everything changed.

I started questioning what I was putting on my lips every day. Ingredients I never paid attention to before suddenly mattered. I realized how easily we trust products without truly knowing how they affect us.

At the same time, I noticed something else — there were very limited options in India for lipsticks that were vegan, cruelty-free, and aligned with halal-conscious values, while also being gentle and suitable for everyday use.

That gap stayed with me.

Tayyib Co. was created from that exact need — not just for myself, but for anyone who has ever faced similar issues and felt like they had to choose between beauty and comfort.

Our goal is simple:
To create lip products that feel right — comfortable, thoughtful, and made with care.

We believe beauty should never come at the cost of your well-being. It should enhance you, not harm you.

Every product we aim to create is rooted in that intention — to be kind to your lips, and true to you.

This isn’t just a brand.
It’s a response to a problem many of us didn’t realize we shared.

TAYYIB CO. — Kind to Lips. True to You.
